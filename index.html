2Upload rules

The rules of uploading

24h

Live within 24 hours

Every delivered ad is live within 24 hours. Nothing gets missed.

≤10

Ads per ad set

10 is the standard, 15 only in the worst case. Over 10, start a second ad set.

1 LP

Ad matches its LP

One landing page per ad set, and every ad in it was made for that page.

3Who uploads what

Two lanes, never shared

Pod owner NER · SUD · ACH
  • Checks the ads: right product, right LP, specs OK
  • Uploads into their own test campaigns
  • Bea takes the Lem ads, Ryan the non-Lem
Cass Every other agency
  • Haseeb / Shabir editors · Heavecorp · Eyeball · Attentional · IG posts / whitelisting · Evrim (trial) · Krave (ends 31 Oct)
  • Checks the ads, splits each batch by product and language
  • Uploads into the pod owner's campaigns, using the structure Bea, Ryan or Neil set up

Pod owner sets the structure, Cass fills it. Each pod owner keeps a test campaign ready for every agency that sends them ads. Cass adds ad sets to it and never has to ask where an ad goes.

The win-and-scale loop

Winners campaign (CBO)

  • One campaign per product × LP. Owned by the pod owner for that product.
  • New ad set every week for that week's winners.
  • Reuse the post ID of the winning test ad so likes and comments carry over.
5Blockers

What stops us going faster?

How much the loop produces = ads tested per week × hit rate × how fast a winner gets scaled. Every manual step on the way slows one of those down. These are the slow spots and what could take them away, in the order an ad hits them.

BlockerWhy it slows usWhat could remove it
1 · Ads uploadEvery ad is built by hand, so volume is capped by people's hoursBulk upload from a labelled folder through the Meta API, with names filled in automatically
2 · Segmenting adsMixed batches have to be sorted by product, language and LP before anything goes liveAgencies label files on delivery. AI tags whatever arrives unlabelled
3 · Checking adsProduct, claims, policy and LP fit are checked by eye. Rejections cost daysAn AI pre-check before upload (we already have a creative QC agent)
4 · Calling winnersNo set thresholds, so every call waits on a person reading dashboardsMomo sets the thresholds, then a daily automatic list of kill / watch / winner per pod
5 · VariationsSteps 2 and 4 wait days on an agency round-tripMake the first variations in-house with AI (hooks, copy, statics) and keep agencies for new concepts
6 · LocalizationEvery English winner has to be translated and re-cut by handAI translation and dubbing, with Neil checking the result
7 · New LPsStep 5 is one person's build timePre-lander templates that AI fills from the winning angle, with Rahul reviewing
8 · Creative reportPut together by hand every weekBuilt automatically from consistent ad names

Measure before fixing: hours from delivery to live, ads tested per week per pod, and days from launch to call. The step with the worst number is the one to fix first. Question for the team: what else slows you down day to day?
